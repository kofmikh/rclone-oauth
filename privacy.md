# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This OAuth application is used solely by its owner to connect
rclone on their own computer to their Google Drive.

## Data access and use

Depending on the permissions granted, rclone can access file
contents and metadata and create, modify or delete files.
This access is used only to provide the Google Drive connection
requested by the owner.

## Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the owner's local
rclone configuration. File contents may also be cached locally.

This application does not operate a separate server collecting
Google Drive data. It does not sell Google user data or share it
with advertising or analytics providers. Data is transferred
between the owner's computer and Google to provide the connection.

## Retention and revocation

Local credentials and cached files remain until the owner removes
them. The owner can revoke access through their Google Account
permissions settings and remove the local rclone configuration
and cache.
